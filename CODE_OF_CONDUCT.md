# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link and applies to every space of the project:
issues, pull requests, discussions and any event or channel run for it.

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- No publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at support@clipy.online.
Every report is read and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban anyone
from the project, temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
